Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 9 October 2026

You can delete your DTT Ireland account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the DTT Ireland app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Under Support, tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer use the app, email blue0orbit@gmail.com from the address on your DTT Ireland account, or tell us that address, and ask us to delete the account. We will confirm within 5 business days, and always within one month.

What Is Deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email address, profile picture and user ID)
  • Your profile, XP, streaks and achievements
  • All study progress: answers, scores, mock exams, bookmarks, flagged questions and spaced-repetition data
  • Scenario Mode and Hazard Perception results, and the items you unlocked
  • Your motorcycle study progress
  • Your app settings, such as language and reminders
  • Our record of which Pro features your account held, and your user ID in our purchase records

What Is Kept

  • Purchase records: the product, its Google Play purchase token, its status (such as paid, pending or refunded) and a few technical details of the purchase, such as whether it was a test purchase. We keep them for up to 6 years, so that a purchase cannot be claimed again by another account, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. When your account is deleted, we remove your user ID from these records, add the date of that change to them, and delete our record of which Pro features your account held. We also keep a code made from the deleted account's user ID with a one-way function, which cannot be turned back into the user ID, and the date it was made. We keep it only so that our purchase server never creates records for that account again.
  • Analytics data, which Google keeps for up to 14 months, and crash reports, which it keeps for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play's own record of your purchase, which Google keeps under its own policies.
  • If you have used our web app: the question service's records, linked to your user ID only (the questions it has given your account, daily counts and mock exams). The question service is not told when an account is deleted, so before deleting your account you can remove these records yourself in the web app (Account → Delete what our server keeps) or ask us by email. Otherwise they expire: daily counts after about 35 days, mock exams after about 90 days, and the records of questions given to your account once it has gone a year without use. Progress the web app saved in your browser stays on that device until you delete it in the web app (Progress → Delete progress on this device) or clear the site's data in your browser. During the private beta, the list of invited accounts also keeps your user ID until we remove it; email us to have it removed. See Our Web App in the privacy policy.

Timeline

  • Your account is deleted, and you are signed out, straight away.
  • Your profile and study data are removed from our database straight away. Google, which hosts our database, removes any remaining copies from its backup systems within 180 days.

Your Purchases

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund a purchase. Pro Car belongs to the Google account that paid for it, so Restore purchases in the app, on a device signed in to that Google account, brings it back. Pro Motorcycle, Pro Both and the upgrades stay linked to the account you deleted, so they are not restored automatically to a new account; email us if you need help with this. Your study progress cannot be recovered after deletion.
