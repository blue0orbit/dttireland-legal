Questions

Before you start

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one: it keeps your daily allowance and your Pro. Sign in with Google, or with an email address and a password. If you already use the DTT Ireland app, use the same account.

Is my progress synced with the phone app?

Not yet. Your account and Pro are shared, but your practice progress is saved in the browser you use, so keep studying in the same browser.

Which languages?

Questions and explanations come in 9 languages; choose yours in Account in the web app. The menus are in English for now.

Is this the official RSA test?

No. DTT Ireland is an independent study app, not affiliated with the RSA, NDLS or Prometric.
