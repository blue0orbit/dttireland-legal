Learner permits

After the theory test: how to get your first learner permit in Ireland (2026)

From your theory test pass to a learner permit in your hand: what NDLS asks for, the fee, applying online or in person, and what you can do next.

Passing the driver theory test does not let you drive yet. Your next step is to apply to the National Driver Licence Service (NDLS) for your first learner permit, and you have two years from the date of your test to do it. First-time applicants usually need an eyesight report, and the fee is €45. You can apply online with a Public Services Card and a verified MyGovID account, or in person at an NDLS centre by appointment. Checked on 14 October 2026 against the official pages listed at the end. DTT Ireland is an independent study app, not the RSA or NDLS.

Here is the whole journey for a first car or motorcycle permit. Each step is explained below.

Your theory test pass: two years to apply

When the test ends, your pass or fail result appears on screen, and theorytest.ie emails you a link to a score report. A pass is for one theory test category (BW covers car and tractor, AM covers motorcycles and mopeds), so make sure it matches the vehicle you want to learn on. You need a theory test pass in the same category to apply for a first learner permit.

The pass doesn’t last for ever: your certificate expires two years after your test date. If you haven’t applied to NDLS for your first learner permit within those two years, theorytest.ie says you will have to sit the test again and pay for it again. Don’t leave it to the last few weeks: a missing document or a late eyesight report can hold an application up.

Check your age too. You must be at least 17 to hold a car (category B) learner permit. That is separate from the theory test itself, where a minimum age of 15 applies to appointments from 1 November 2026.

What NDLS asks for

The exact list depends on your situation and on how you apply, so read the NDLS page for your case before you start. This is what it covers for a first learner permit:

Theory test passEveryone applying for a first permit in a categoryIn the category you are applying for, taken within the last two years.

The eyesight report. It is required with a first learner permit application, unless you send a medical report instead (see below). You sign the form in front of the optometrist or doctor who fills it in. The form records whether you need glasses or contact lenses to meet the eyesight standard for driving. If you do, your permit carries code 01 (sight correction), which means you must wear them to drive. The optician or doctor may charge for the report; it is not part of the NDLS fee.

The medical report. The application asks health questions, for example about conditions or regular medication that could affect your driving. A yes to any of them means you need a medical report from a doctor. Everyone applying for a truck or bus permit needs one as well. The doctor must see you in person, and you sign the form in front of them. If you send a medical report, you don’t need a separate eyesight report unless your doctor says you do. If you are unsure, ask your GP before you apply rather than guessing.

Your right to live in Ireland. Since 19 May 2025, the law says every learner permit application must come with evidence of a legal right to live in the State. The list includes an Irish or EU/EEA/Swiss/UK passport, an EU/EEA/Swiss national ID card, and a Public Services Card that shows one of those nationalities. If you are not a citizen of one of those countries, the list includes a current immigration registration certificate (the Irish Residence Permit), shown with your passport or Public Services Card.

Apply online or at an NDLS centre

Online. NDLS takes first learner permit applications online. To start, you need a Public Services Card and a verified MyGovID account; the whole application happens online, with nothing to print and no centre to visit. You can upload your eyesight report during the application or post it to the address in your confirmation email. If the photo on your Public Services Card is more than 10 years old, you can’t apply online until you renew the card.

In person. Book an appointment at an NDLS centre on ndls.ie; centres don’t take walk-ins. Bring the original documents from the table above, plus your eyesight report and, if you need one, your medical report. You don’t need a paper application form, unless you want to apply in Irish, which needs the Irish application form. Staff check your documents at the counter, and your photo and signature are taken there. NDLS doesn’t accept photocopies of photo ID.

We are not NDLS or the RSA.

DTT Ireland can’t apply for a permit for you, book NDLS appointments or check your documents. Apply only on ndls.ie or at an NDLS centre, and never send identity documents to anyone else to “speed up” an application.

The fee and how long the permit lasts

NDLS learner permit fees (checked 14 October 2026)
ApplicationFee
Learner permit (first or renewal)€45
Adding a category to a learner permit€35
Replacement (duplicate) learner permit€35

You pay by card; at an NDLS centre, Google Pay, Apple Pay or a Payzone voucher also work. If you are 70 or older, a first or renewal permit is free, but the €35 fee for a replacement permit still applies. The €45 has applied since 1 January 2025; before that it was €35, so ignore older guides that still quote the old fee.

A learner permit is normally granted for two years. Your first permit in a category carries code 991, which is what “first learner permit” means on the card. Keep the card safe: you sign in to MyEDT with its details, and you need a valid permit to sit the driving test.

After the permit: EDT, IBT and the driving test

Learning to drive a car. From the day your permit is valid, you can drive only under the learner rules. You must be accompanied at all times by an Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) or someone who has held a full licence for the same category for at least two years, and you must display L-plates. You can’t drive on motorways or tow a trailer. Our guide to L plates, N plates and penalty points explains these rules and the fines.

You also start Essential Driver Training (EDT): 12 one-hour sessions with an ADI, recorded in your logbook and in MyEDT. Our EDT guide covers the sessions, the sponsor and how MyEDT works. Before you can sit the driving test, you must have held your first learner permit for at least six months and finished EDT. You apply for the driving test through MyRoadSafety.

Learning to ride a motorcycle. You must complete Initial Basic Training (IBT) with an approved instructor before you ride on a public road, and carry your IBT certificate when you ride. Learner riders wear a yellow tabard with an L and can’t carry a passenger. Our IBT guide helps you choose a course and compare quotes.

What changes on 1 November 2026

New rules for learner permits start on 1 November 2026. They don’t change how you get your first permit, but they limit how long you can keep renewing without taking the driving test:

  • a third or fourth learner permit will need a driving test taken in the previous two years;
  • a fourth permit will last only one year, and there will be no fifth permit (unless you were medically restricted);
  • if your permit has been expired for two years or more, you start again, theory test included.

In practice, plan to take your driving test while you are on your first or second permit. Our guide to the learner permit changes in November 2026 has the details and a checklist for your own dates.

Common questions

Can I drive as soon as I pass the theory test?

No. The theory test is only the first step. You need a learner permit first, and then you must follow the learner rules, such as being accompanied and displaying L-plates.

How long do I have to apply after passing?

Two years from the date of your theory test. After that, you have to pass the test again before you can apply for a first permit.

Do I need a medical report?

Only if you answer yes to a health question on the application or you are applying for a truck or bus permit. If you send one, you don’t need a separate eyesight report unless your doctor says so; otherwise, first-time applicants need an eyesight report.

Can I apply online without a Public Services Card?

No. Online applications need a Public Services Card and a verified MyGovID account. Without them, book an appointment at an NDLS centre.

Can DTT Ireland apply for my permit or check my documents?

No. We are an independent study app. Only NDLS handles learner permit applications.
