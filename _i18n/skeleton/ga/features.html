Everything You Need to Pass

From real RSA questions to 60fps driving scenarios — DTT Ireland is the most complete Irish theory test app.

Core Feature

805 Real RSA Questions

Practice every question from the official RSA question bank. Covering all 8 exam categories, each question includes a detailed explanation so you understand the reasoning — not just the answer.

  • Road Signs (regulatory, warning, information)
  • Rules of the Road — junctions, roundabouts, overtaking
  • Traffic Lights and pedestrian crossings
  • Speed Limits — national, regional, local
  • Hazard Awareness on Irish roads
  • Vehicle Mechanics — lights, tyres, basic checks
  • First Aid & Safety — emergency procedures, seatbelts
  • Environment & Economy — eco-driving, emissions
📚
Exam Simulation

Full Mock Exam Mode

Simulate the real RSA theory test exactly — 40 questions, 45-minute timer, same pass threshold. Train under real conditions so there are no surprises on test day.

  • 40 questions drawn from all 8 categories
  • 45-minute countdown with 5-minute warning
  • Pass mark: 35/40 (87.5%) — same as real RSA exam
  • Detailed results with pass/fail verdict
  • Post-exam review of every answer
  • Track your exam readiness score over time
📝
Unique Feature

Driving Scenario Simulations

The only Irish theory test app with real-time driving scenarios. A 60fps physics engine evaluates your decision-making at real road situations — not just your ability to memorise answers.

  • T-junction gap finding
  • Pedestrian crossing reactions
  • Roundabout entry timing
  • Narrow road oncoming traffic
  • Speed zone hazard recognition
  • Straight road merging and lane changes
  • Time-to-collision (TTC) feedback chart
  • XP rewards for decisive, safe decisions
🎯
Smart Learning

Spaced Repetition System

DTT Ireland uses an SM-2-inspired spaced repetition algorithm — the same proven technique behind Anki and Duolingo — to automatically resurface questions you get wrong at optimal intervals.

  • Adaptive intervals: 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days
  • Wrong answers prioritised in review sessions
  • Up to 20 due questions per review session
  • Background sync via WorkManager
  • Streak reminders and daily challenge push notifications
🧠
Progress Tracking

Progress Dashboard & Achievements

Know exactly where you stand. The Progress tab shows your predicted exam score, per-category mastery, weekly study trend, and an XP streak system that keeps you coming back.

  • Predicted exam readiness score
  • Category mastery (0–100% per category)
  • Daily streak counter and longest streak
  • Weekly study activity bar chart
  • 10 unlockable achievements (badges)
  • Bookmark questions for later review
  • Mistakes review after every session
📈
Multilingual

9 Languages — Including RTL

DTT Ireland is the only Irish theory test app that supports 9 languages — including right-to-left Arabic and Urdu. All questions, answers, and explanations are fully translated.

  • 🇮🇪 Irish (Gaeilge)
  • 🇵🇱 Polish
  • 🇵🇹 Portuguese
  • 🇺🇦 Ukrainian
  • 🇸🇦 Arabic (RTL layout)
