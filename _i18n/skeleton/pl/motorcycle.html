Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Where do I find motorcycle questions?

Update DTT Ireland through Google Play, then choose Motorcycle using the selector on the home screen.

Does my existing Car Pro purchase include Motorcycle?

No. Choose Pro Motorcycle or an available upgrade to Pro Both. Check the purchase screen for the current price and what is included.

Does the app replace IBT or the official test?

No. DTT Ireland is an independent study aid. Book official services separately and confirm your requirements with the RSA and NDLS.

Will my official test be in the same language as the app?

Not necessarily. App study languages and official test assistance are different services. Confirm the language options for your specific test when booking.
