Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DTT Ireland is an independent app developed by blue0orbit. It is not affiliated with the Road Safety Authority (RSA) or any government body.

1. Who We Are

DTT Ireland is an independent study app developed and run by blue0orbit, an independent developer based in Ireland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Our developer details are also shown on the app's Google Play listing.

DTT Ireland is not affiliated with the Road Safety Authority (RSA) or any government body.

If you have any questions about your data, email blue0orbit@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Account: when you sign in with Google, Facebook or an email address, we receive your name, email address, profile picture (Google and Facebook only) and a user ID. Your password is handled by Google Firebase and is never visible to us.

Study activity: your answers, scores, mock exams, streaks, XP, achievements, bookmarks, flagged questions, Scenario Mode and Hazard Perception results, the items you have unlocked, and your app settings (such as language and reminders), so your progress syncs across your devices.

Purchases: the product you bought, its order number and its purchase token, so our purchase server can check the purchase with Google Play, restore it and record which Pro features your account holds. When you buy Pro Motorcycle, Pro Both or an upgrade, the app also gives Google Play a code made from your user ID with a one-way function, which links the purchase to your DTT Ireland account. We never see or store your card or payment details.

Usage and device data: from the moment you open the app, we collect app events (such as screens viewed and features used), an app instance ID, your device's advertising ID, your device model, operating system, app version and language, your approximate location (country or region, worked out from your IP address), and crash and performance reports. Once you are signed in, crash reports are linked to your user ID so we can fix problems you run into.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files.

3. How We Use Your Data, and Why

  • To provide the app: creating your account, syncing your progress, verifying and restoring purchases, and remembering your settings. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To keep the app working and secure: crash and performance reports, preventing fraud, and protecting our servers from abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a reliable and secure app.
  • To understand and improve the app: usage analytics. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app.
  • To fund the free version: showing ads, and measuring our own advertising (for example on Facebook and Instagram). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in funding the app. Google shows you personalised ads only if you agree in its consent form.
  • To send study reminders, if you turn them on. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • To answer your messages and requests. Legal basis: our legitimate interests and our legal obligations.

You need an account to use the app. You can object to any use based on our legitimate interests at any time (see Your Rights).

4. Advertising and Analytics

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob, and offers optional rewarded video ads from Unity Ads, which you can choose to watch to unlock a single scenario or hazard scene. Pro Car and Pro Both remove the ads. Pro Motorcycle on its own unlocks the motorcycle section and does not remove ads from the car sections.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is offered while you set up the app and, if you have not answered it then, appears the next time you open the app. It decides whether Google may show you personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy Preferences. Unity Ads and Meta may use your advertising ID to personalise and measure ads.

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring start when the app opens. The Meta (Facebook) SDK in the app also records app installs and app opens, together with your advertising ID, so we can measure our advertising on Facebook and Instagram.

You can limit the use of your advertising ID at any time by deleting or resetting it in your phone's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). To object to analytics, email us.

5. Who We Share Data With

We use these service providers. Each receives only what it needs for its job and handles it under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, our purchase server, analytics, crash and performance reports, app integrity checks (Google Play Integrity checks that requests come from the genuine app) and app settings
  • Google AdMob: banner ads
  • Google Play: purchases, purchase checks and refund notices for our purchase server, in-app reviews and update prompts
  • Meta (Facebook): Facebook sign-in, if you choose it, and measurement of our ads
  • Unity Ads: rewarded video ads
  • Google reCAPTCHA: security checks in our web app (see Our Web App)
  • Cloudflare: delivery of question images, delivery and protection of our website, and hosting of our web app and its question service (it sees your IP address)

We do not sell your personal data.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Our Website

Our website, dttireland.com, uses Google Analytics (through Firebase) to count visits and see which pages are used, but only if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie notice. If you accept, it sets analytics cookies and collects your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view and the site that sent you. Legal basis: your consent. If you reject, no analytics cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; rejecting deletes the analytics cookies. Your choice is remembered in your browser for 12 months. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

The website is delivered through Cloudflare's global network, which also protects it from attacks and abuse. To do this, Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical details of each request, such as the page requested and your browser type. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a fast and secure website.

Most of our pages load their font (Inter) from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and technical details of the request, such as your browser type. A few blog pages that show charts also load a charting script from cdnjs, a service run by Cloudflare. These requests set no cookies and are not part of the cookie choice. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in presenting the website.

Apart from the web app (see Our Web App), the website does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your name or email address.

7. Our Web App

Our web app, at dttireland.com/app/, lets you study in your browser. You sign in with the same account as the DTT Ireland app, using Google or an email address and password, or you can create an account there. Accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, in the same Firebase project as the app, as described in Information We Collect. Firebase keeps you signed in by storing a sign-in token in your browser.

Security checks: to stop automated copying of our question bank, the web app uses Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA. While you use the web app, reCAPTCHA assesses your browser to tell genuine use from automated scripts. To do this, Google may set cookies and collect information about your device and browser and how you interact with the page. Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. reCAPTCHA runs only in the web app, not on the rest of our website. These checks are needed to provide the web app securely, so they are not part of the cookie choice described in Our Website.

Hosting: the web app's pages and its question service are hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and technical details of each request to deliver them and to protect them from attacks. The question service also uses your IP address to limit how many requests can be made in a short time. It does not store your IP address in its database.

Question service records: the question service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database, picks your questions and marks your mock exams. It keeps these records, linked only to your account's user ID, not to your name or email address:

  • The questions it has given your account, and the day each was first given. These records limit how quickly the question bank can be copied. You can delete them at any time in the web app (Account → Delete what our server keeps). Otherwise they are deleted automatically once your account has gone a year without new questions and 35 days without any use of the web app.
  • How many questions, new questions and mock exams it has given your account each day, so it can apply the daily limits. These counts are deleted after about 35 days.
  • Each mock exam it put together for you: the questions, their order and language, when it started, its time limit and when you handed it in. The question service also counts these to apply the limits of your plan: without Pro Motorcycle, for example, your account gets one free Motorcycle mock exam. These are deleted after about 90 days.

Pro status: to apply the limits of your plan, the question service checks whether your account has Pro by reading your account's Pro record in our Firebase database, using your own sign-in. It keeps the answer in memory for up to five minutes and does not store it.

Private beta: while the web app is open only to invited learners, the question service also keeps a list of the user IDs of invited accounts, with the date each was added and a short note. Delete what our server keeps (in the web app) does not remove this entry; email us to have it removed.

Your answers to practice questions are checked in your browser and are not sent to the question service. When you hand in a mock exam, your answers are sent to it only to mark the exam: it sends back your result and does not keep your answers.

Progress on your device: your practice answers, mock exam results and the progress the web app shows you are saved only in your browser's local storage on that device, separately for each account. They are not synced with the DTT Ireland app yet. You can delete them at any time in the web app (Progress → Delete progress on this device) or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Legal basis: performing our contract with you, to sign you in, give you questions, mark your mock exams and apply the limits of your plan; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse, for App Check and reCAPTCHA, request limits, daily limits and the records of questions given to your account.

Deleting your account: the web app does not have its own Delete Account option yet, so delete your account in the DTT Ireland app (Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing us. The question service is not told when an account is deleted, so before you delete your account you can remove its records yourself in the web app (Account → Delete what our server keeps), or email blue0orbit@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete them. If you do neither, they still expire: the daily counts after about 35 days, mock exams after about 90 days, and the records of questions given to your account once it has gone a year without use.

8. Huawei AppGallery Version

The version of DTT Ireland on Huawei AppGallery uses the same services described in this policy, except Google AdMob and Google Play. Any purchases in that version are handled by Huawei AppGallery under Huawei's own terms.

9. Security, Retention and Deletion

Your data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with Google Firebase, where access rules mean each account can read only its own data.

We keep your account and study data for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab (Delete Account), or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes your profile and study data from our database straight away. Google, which hosts our database, removes any remaining copies from its backup systems within 180 days.

Some records are kept after that:

  • Purchase records: the product, its Google Play purchase token, its status (such as paid, pending or refunded) and a few technical details of the purchase, such as whether it was a test purchase. We keep them for up to 6 years, so that a purchase cannot be claimed again by another account, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. When your account is deleted, we remove your user ID from these records, add the date of that change to them, and delete our record of which Pro features your account held. We also keep a code made from the deleted account's user ID with a one-way function, which cannot be turned back into the user ID, and the date it was made. We keep it only so that our purchase server never creates records for that account again.
  • Analytics data, which Google keeps for up to 14 months, and crash reports, which it keeps for up to 90 days.
  • Google Play's own record of your purchase, which Google keeps under its own policies.
  • If you have used our web app, the question service's records (see Our Web App). The daily counts are deleted after about 35 days and mock exams after about 90 days; the records of questions given to your account are deleted when you ask (in the web app or by email) or once the account has gone a year without use. Progress saved in your browser stays on that device until you delete it.

10. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Have your data erased
  • Restrict how we use it
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Object to our use of your data based on our legitimate interests, including analytics and ad measurement
  • Withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting what happened before

To use any of these rights, email blue0orbit@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie), or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

We do not make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

11. Children

The app is meant for learner drivers and riders aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child under 16 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the date at the top, and where we can, we will tell you about important changes in the app before they take effect.

This policy is available in several languages. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.
