Studying for Ireland’s theory test in Chinese

Simplified Chinese study support, official exam voiceover options and a practical revision routine.

Study language and exam language are different choices

DTT Ireland now includes Simplified Chinese (简体中文). It joins English, Irish, Polish, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Arabic, Urdu and Romanian. Update the Android app and choose Chinese in its language settings.

Chinese in the official exam

The official service lists Simplified Chinese among its 21 voiceover languages for the BW cars, tractors and work vehicles exam. This is a voiceover option, not a claim that every category has a fully translated written exam. See the official voiceover announcement.

Check official assistance options when booking. Do not assume BW support also applies to motorcycle tests. Confirm your category and needs directly with the test service.

A bilingual study routine

  1. Start with a short topic session in the language you understand best.
  2. Write a glossary of unfamiliar road terms, with English beside the Chinese meaning.
  3. Read the explanation, not only the correct answer.
  4. Return to missed questions the following day.
  5. Use mixed practice to check understanding beyond familiar question order.

Keep a separate list of signs and terms you encounter outside the app. If a translation leaves a rule unclear, check official guidance or ask your instructor before applying it on the road.

给中文学习者的小提示

学习时可以使用简体中文理解题目，同时记录常见的英文交通术语。预约正式考试时，请在官方网站确认考试类别和语音辅助选项。应用内的语言设置不会自动更改正式考试的语言。

Ready to arrange your exam? Use the official booking service. Keep your confirmation and check the identification instructions before test day.

DTT Ireland is an independent study app, not the RSA, NDLS or the official test booking service. Check official guidance before booking or relying on a rule.
