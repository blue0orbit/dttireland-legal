Motorcycle theory test in Ireland: where to start

A practical route through category selection, theory booking, learner permits and motorcycle training.

Start with the right category

Before buying study material or booking, use the RSA category guide. Choose the route that fits your age, existing licence and intended motorcycle. Do not assume a car entitlement covers the motorcycle you want to ride.

Book the correct theory test

Use the official theory booking service and check the motorcycle option and identification requirements. Theory booking and the practical riding test are separate processes. Your practice-app language preference does not automatically choose a language or assistance option for the official exam.

After theory: permit and training

Check the first learner-permit steps. Motorcycle learners need Initial Basic Training delivered by an RSA-approved IBT instructor at an approved centre. Read the RSA’s IBT guidance before arranging training; the course and progression route depend on your category and circumstances.

Organise your revision

Use motorcycle practice rather than relying only on car questions. Build a list of topics you find difficult: protective equipment, observation, positioning, braking and road surfaces. Read explanations after each answer and revisit errors on another day.

Alternate short topic sessions with mixed practice. If you remember a question’s wording but cannot explain the answer, return to the underlying rule. Treat practice scores as a study signal, not a guarantee of passing.

Motorcycle study in DTT Ireland

The latest Android update adds a dedicated Motorcycle choice alongside Car. See motorcycle practice and access options. Update through Google Play if you do not yet see the selector.

Your practical test

Start with the RSA application guide. Check training and vehicle requirements with the RSA and your instructor before accepting an appointment.

DTT Ireland is an independent study app, not the RSA, NDLS or the official test booking service. Check official guidance before booking or relying on a rule.
