A small check saves a wasted trip

Before you confirm a booking

  1. Match the service to your goal. Check the exam or application category.
  2. Read the requirements. Confirm identification, eligibility and required documents.
  3. Save the confirmation. Keep the date, centre and arrival instructions together.

DTT Ireland provides study tools and links. We do not take official bookings or collect application documents. Fees, availability and requirements are set by the linked services.
