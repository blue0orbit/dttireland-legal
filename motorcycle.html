Learner guides · Updated 28 September 2026

Motorcycle theory practice in DTT Ireland

Choose Car or Motorcycle in the latest Android app and focus on the category you want to study.

Two study paths, easy to find

Use the Car / Motorcycle selector to choose your study path. Motorcycle questions have their own practice content rather than being mixed into car revision. Update through Google Play if you do not yet see the selector.

Study in your language

Simplified Chinese joins the app’s nine language choices. Read about language support and our Chinese study guide.

Choose the access you need

Choose Pro Car, Pro Motorcycle or Pro Both. Check the price and included features displayed in the app before purchasing. Existing Car access does not automatically include the Motorcycle pack.

Plan your next step

Our motorcycle getting-started guide links to official booking and training guidance. App practice does not replace the official exam or IBT.

DTT Ireland is an independent study app, not the RSA, NDLS or the official test booking service. Check official guidance before booking or relying on a rule.
