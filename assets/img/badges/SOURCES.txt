====================================================

(play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges, which now redirects to Google's Partner Marketing Hub).
Only the transparent margin around each badge was trimmed; every badge pixel is unchanged
(checked pixel by pixel against Google's file).

Language pages: pl, pt (Brazilian), ro, uk and zh use their own badge (_i18n/build.py PLAY_BADGE).
ga, ar and ur use the English badge: Google has no Irish badge file, and its public Arabic and
Urdu files still show the old (pre-2022) Google Play logo, which Google's rules say not to use.
The current Arabic and Urdu badges are only on the Partner Marketing Hub, behind an agreement
the owner must accept himself:
https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/?folder=86641

Google's rules followed (Partner Marketing Hub, read 10 Oct 2026): at least 28 px high on screen
(we show 52 px); clear space of a quarter of the badge height (13 px) around it; no change to
colour, elements or proportions; the Google Play badge the same size as or larger than other store
badges. Pages that show it carry the line "Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of
Google LLC."

Huawei AppGallery: appgallery-badge.svg in assets/img is still our own drawing of the badge.
Huawei's official badge can only be downloaded after signing in to AppGallery Connect
(All services > Distribute > AppGallery Download > Badge links > "Download badges of all languages",
https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/appgallery-connect-guides/agdlink-getlink-agc-0000001164321881).
Replace it with the official file once the owner has downloaded it.
